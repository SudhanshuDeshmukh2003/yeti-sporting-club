About

High altitude.
Higher spirit.

Born in the high valleys of Leh, Yeti Sporting Club plays with the grit of the Himalaya—thin air, hard pitches, and a community that shows up. We train young players, compete locally, and carry Ladakh’s football spirit onto every field.
